Contact

Thanks for using ender3v2.com. If you've got feedback, a settings suggestion, or you've spotted an error, drop us an email.

Email
hello@ender3v2.com

What to Get in Touch About

  • Settings feedback — if a recommended setting didn't work for you, tell us what filament and what happened
  • Filament suggestions — if there's a filament type you'd like us to add (PCTG, ASA-X, glass-fibre composites, etc.)
  • Bug reports — anything broken on the site
  • Content ideas — additional guides, troubleshooting topics, or upgrade reviews you'd like to see
  • Corrections — if something is technically wrong, we want to know

What We Can't Help With

We're a small reference site — we can't troubleshoot your specific printer issues over email, recommend specific filament brands, or diagnose hardware problems remotely. For these, the r/ender3v2 and r/3Dprinting subreddits, plus the official Creality support, are your best bets.

Response Time

We aim to reply to every email within 3-5 working days. Please be patient — this is a hobby project, not a business.
